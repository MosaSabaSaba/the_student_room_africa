Unit 3E

Algebra and Graphs — Graphs

1. The graph of y=x2−5x+5 is drawn for values of x between 0 and 5. On each axis 1 cm represents 1 unit.

(a) Use the graph to estimate, correct to one decimal place, the solutions of the equation x2−5x+5=0.

(b) By drawing the tangent at the point (1, 1), estimate the gradient of the curve at this point.

(J81/1/28)

2. The curve y=(x+3)(x−2) cuts the x-axis at the points A and C, and the y-axis at B.

(a) Write down the coordinates of the points A, B and C.

(b) Find the equation of the line of symmetry of the curve.

(N81/1/23)

3. On the axes in the answer space, sketch and clearly label the graphs of

(a) x+y=0,

(b) y=x3.

(J82/1/20)

4. The curve y=px3+8 intersects the y-axis at A and passes through the point (−2, −32).

(a) Write down the coordinates of A.

(b) Calculate the value of p.

(N82/1/18)
