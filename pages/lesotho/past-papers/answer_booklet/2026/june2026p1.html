1.
(a) 1⁄5 (or 0.2)
(b) 2
Show workings

(a) 2⁄5 ÷ 2

Change division to multiplication by the reciprocal:

2⁄5 × 1⁄2

Cancel the common factor of 2:

= 1⁄5

(b) [0.5(10 + 6)] ÷ 4

Brackets first: 10 + 6 = 16

Multiply by 0.5 (same as halving): 0.5 × 16 = 8

Divide by 4: 8 ÷ 4 = 2

5.
b = 2
Show workings

Distance between centres X and Y is 1 + 1 = 2 units.

Distance between centres X and Z is 1 + 1 = 2 units.

Using Pythagoras on the right-angled triangle YXZ:

YZ² = XY² + XZ² = 2² + 2² = 8

YZ = √8

The segment YZ passes through P and Q, with YP = 1 and QZ = 1.

PQ = YZ − YP − QZ = √8 − 1 − 1 = √8 − 2

So b = 2

n = 18 ÷ 40

= 0.45 (or 9⁄20)

0.2 + m + 0.45 + 0.1 = 1

m = 0.25

Number of students = 0.25 × 40 = 10

9.
(a) Shown below
(b) x = 5
Show workings

(a) Factorise as a difference of squares:

[(3x − y) − (x − 5)][(3x − y) + (x − 5)] = 0

= (2x − y + 5)(4x − y − 5) = 0

Since 2x − y + 5 = 0, the product is zero, so

4x − y − 5 = 0 satisfies the equation. ∎

(b) Solve the system:

  1. 2x − y = −5
  2. 4x − y = 5

Subtract (1) from (2): 2x = 10

x = 5

Show workings

PQ = 11 units up, so Q = (0, −3 + 11) = (0, 8)

Parallel lines share gradient m = 2

y = 2x + 8

(b) R is the x-intercept (y = 0): 0 = 2x − 3 → R = (1.5, 0)

Midpoint of QR = ((0 + 1.5)/2, (8 + 0)/2)

= (0.75, 4) or (3⁄4, 4)

Show workings

= 2 − (−1)

= 3 °C

(b) Mean = Σ(fx) ÷ Σf

= [ (−1 × 6) + (0 × 3) + (1 × 7) + (2 × 4) ] ÷ (6 + 3 + 7 + 4)

= (−6 + 0 + 7 + 8) ÷ 20

= 9 ÷ 20

= 0.45 °C
