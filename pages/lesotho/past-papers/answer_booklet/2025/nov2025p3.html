1.
(a) Pattern 4: dots = 16, crosses = 14, sum = 30
(b) (i) 137
(ii) n2 + 3n + 2
Show workings

(a) Complete the table for pattern 4.

Dots form the sequence 1, 4, 9, 16, … which is n2.

Crosses form the sequence 5, 8, 11, 14, … which is 3n + 2.

Pattern 4: dots = 42 = 16; crosses = 3(4) + 2 = 14.

Sum = 16 + 14 = 30

(b) (i) Number of crosses needed for pattern 45:

Crosses = 3n + 2 = 3(45) + 2 = 135 + 2 = 137

(b) (ii) The nth term for the sum of crosses and dots:

Sum = n2 + (3n + 2)

= n2 + 3n + 2

2.
(a) M1 810
(b) (i) 5 stacks
(ii) M40
(c) 29 months
Show workings

(a) Balance before deposit = −M180. New balance = M1 630.

Deposit = New balance − Old balance = 1 630 − (−180) = 1 630 + 180 = M1 810

(b) (i) M1 100 paid at M220 per stack.

Number of stacks = 1 100⁄220 = 5

(b) (ii) Reserved M15 000.

Maximum whole stacks = 68 (since 68 × 220 = 14 960 ≤ 15 000 and 69 × 220 = 15 180 > 15 000)

Cost = 68 × 220 = M14 960

Amount remaining = 15 000 − 14 960 = M40

(c) Accumulated amount = M40 000. Company gives 28.95% and keeps the balance.

Employee's share = 0.2895 × 40 000 = M11 580

Balance kept by company = 40 000 − 11 580 = M28 420

Monthly payment = M980

Number of months = 28 420⁄980 = 29 months

3.
(a) (2, 2)
(b) 2x + 3y − 10 = 0
(c) 24 square units
Show workings

Given A(−4, 6), B(8, −2), C(2, −2).

(a) Midpoint of AB:

Midpoint = (−4 + 8⁄2, 6 + (−2)⁄2) = (4⁄2, 4⁄2) = (2, 2)

(b) Equation of line AB in the form ax + by + c = 0:

Gradient = −2 − 6⁄8 − (−4) = −8⁄12 = −2⁄3

Using point A(−4, 6):

y − 6 = −2⁄3(x + 4)

3(y − 6) = −2(x + 4)

3y − 18 = −2x − 8

2x + 3y − 10 = 0

(c) Area of triangle ABC:

Using the coordinate formula:

Area = 1⁄2 | xA(yB − yC) + xB(yC − yA) + xC(yA − yB) |

= 1⁄2 | (−4)(−2 − (−2)) + 8((−2) − 6) + 2(6 − (−2)) |

= 1⁄2 | 0 + 8(−8) + 2(8) |

= 1⁄2 | −64 + 16 |

= 1⁄2 × 48 = 24

4.
(a) 125
(b) (x − 5)(x + 4)
(c) 9.58
(d) 9a + 22⁄20
(e) (i) a = 12
(ii) 39 + 5a⁄17 + a
Show workings

(a) Given a + b = 9 and a2 + b2 = 71.

(a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

92 = 71 + 2ab

81 − 71 = 2ab ⟹ ab = 5

(ab)3 = 53 = 125

(b) Factorise x2 − x − 20.

Two numbers that multiply to −20 and add to −1: −5 and +4.

x2 − x − 20 = (x − 5)(x + 4)

(c) T = 2π√(l⁄g) with l = 22.8, g = 9.8.

T = 2π√(22.8⁄9.8)

= 2π × 1.5253…

= 9.5838…

≈ 9.58 (to 3 s.f.)

(d) Express a + 2⁄4 + a + 3⁄5 as a single fraction.

LCM of 4 and 5 is 20.

= 5(a + 2)⁄20 + 4(a + 3)⁄20

= 5a + 10 + 4a + 12⁄20

= 9a + 22⁄20

(e) (i) Table: days 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 with frequencies 5, 4, 6, 2, a. Total = 17 + a.

Cumulative frequencies: after day 1 = 5, after day 2 = 9, after day 3 = 15, after day 4 = 17, after day 5 = 17 + a.

For the median to equal 3, the middle value must fall in day 3's range (positions 10 to 15).

Middle position = 17 + a + 1⁄2 = 18 + a⁄2

For this to fall between 10 and 15:

10 ≤ 18 + a⁄2 ≤ 15

20 ≤ 18 + a ≤ 30

2 ≤ a ≤ 12

Greatest value of a = 12

(e) (ii) Expression for the mean in terms of a.

Sum of (day × frequency) = 1(5) + 2(4) + 3(6) + 4(2) + 5(a)

= 5 + 8 + 18 + 8 + 5a = 39 + 5a

Total frequency = 17 + a

Mean = 39 + 5a⁄17 + a

7.
(a) 11 cm
(b) (i) 204.2 cm²
(ii) 6.75 cm
Show workings

(a) Find the length of the side of square ABCD.

The rectangle has area 15 cm² and horizontal width 5 cm.

Height of rectangle = 15⁄5 = 3 cm

Let the square's side = x.

Horizontal distance from the rectangle's right edge to vertex B = x − 5.

Vertical distance from the rectangle's top edge to vertex B = x − 3.

Applying Pythagoras to the right-angled triangle with hypotenuse 10 cm:

(x − 5)2 + (x − 3)2 = 102

x2 − 10x + 25 + x2 − 6x + 9 = 100

2x2 − 16x + 34 = 100

2x2 − 16x − 66 = 0

x2 − 8x − 33 = 0

(x − 11)(x + 3) = 0

x = 11 (since x > 0)

(b) (i) Area of the container in contact with water.

Cylinder diameter = 10 cm, so radius r = 5 cm.

Water depth = 4 cm.

Area in contact = base area + curved surface up to water level

= πr2 + 2πrh

= π(25) + 2π(5)(4)

= 25π + 40π = 65π

= 65 × 3.142 = 204.23 cm2

≈ 204.2 cm2

(b) (ii) New water level after immersing a 6 cm cube.

Volume of cube = 63 = 216 cm3

Base area of cylinder = π(52) = 25π = 78.55 cm2

Rise in water level = 216⁄78.55 = 2.75 cm

New water level = 4 + 2.75 = 6.75 cm

10.
(a) 150°
(b) 6.43 m
(c) 8.15 m
Show workings

Given: BX = 10 m, ∠OBX = 50°, ∠XCO = 60°, ∠BXA = 20°.

(a) Find the size of angle PXC.

In right-angled triangle XOC: ∠XCO = 60°, ∠XOC = 90°.

∠OXC = 180° − 90° − 60° = 30°

P, X, O are collinear with XO and XP forming a straight line.

∠PXC = 180° − ∠OXC = 180° − 30° = 150°

(b) Length BO in right triangle BXO.

BX = 10 m is the hypotenuse, BO is adjacent to ∠OBX = 50°.

cos(50°) = BO⁄BX

BO = 10 × cos(50°)

= 10 × 0.6428 = 6.43 m

(c) Length AX.

In triangle ABX: ∠ABX = 50° (since A, B, O are collinear), ∠BXA = 20°.

∠BAX = 180° − 50° − 20° = 110°

By Sine Rule:

AX⁄sin(50°) = BX⁄sin(110°)

AX = 10 × sin(50°)⁄sin(110°)

= 10 × 0.7660⁄0.9397

= 8.15 m

11.
(a) x = 8, y = 2, z = 18
(b) (i) 12
(ii) 32
(c) L ∩ M′
Show workings

40 people total. n(M) = 10, n(L) = 20, neither = 12.

n(M ∪ L) = 40 − 12 = 28

n(M ∩ L) = n(M) + n(L) − n(M ∪ L) = 10 + 20 − 28 = 2

So y = 2

n(M only) = 10 − 2 = 8 ⟹ x = 8

n(L only) = 20 − 2 = 18 ⟹ z = 18

(b) (i) n(M ∪ L)′:

Number of people not in M or L = 12

(b) (ii) n(M′ ∪ L):

M′ = people not in M = 18 + 12 = 30

The only people NOT in M′ ∪ L are those in M only = 8

n(M′ ∪ L) = 40 − 8 = 32

(c) Landline only = L ∩ M′

12.
(a) (i) Translation by vector (−6, −2)
(ii) 90° clockwise rotation about (2, 1)
(b) Requires drawing — apply the enlargement on the grid
Show workings

(a) (i) Describe a single transformation that maps A onto B.

Triangle A vertices: (1, 3), (3, 3), (3, 6).

Triangle B vertices: (−5, 1), (−3, 1), (−3, 4).

Each vertex of B = corresponding vertex of A + (−6, −2).

Check: (1, 3) + (−6, −2) = (−5, 1) ✓

So the transformation is a translation by the vector (−6, −2).

(a) (ii) Describe a single transformation that maps A onto C.

Triangle C vertices: (4, 0), (7, 0), (4, 2).

Using the 90° clockwise rotation rule about (a, b): (x, y) → (a + (y − b), b − (x − a))

We need (3, 3) → (4, 0):

a + 3 − b = 4 ⟹ a − b = 1

b − 3 + a = 0 ⟹ a + b = 3

Solving: a = 2, b = 1

Check other vertices:

(3, 6) → (2 + 5, 1 − 1) = (7, 0) ✓

(1, 3) → (2 + 2, 1 + 1) = (4, 2) ✓

So the transformation is a 90° clockwise rotation about the point (2, 1).

(b) Enlarge B with centre (1, 1) and scale factor 1⁄2.

For each vertex: new position = centre + k(vertex − centre)

(−5, 1) → (1 + 0.5(−6), 1 + 0.5(0)) = (−2, 1)

(−3, 1) → (1 + 0.5(−4), 1 + 0.5(0)) = (−1, 1)

(−3, 4) → (1 + 0.5(−4), 1 + 0.5(3)) = (−1, 2.5)

Draw the triangle with these new vertices on the grid.
