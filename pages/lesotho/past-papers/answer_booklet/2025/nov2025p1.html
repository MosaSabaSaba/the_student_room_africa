1.
38⁄15 (or 53⁄15)
Show workings

Work out 11⁄5 + 21⁄3.

1. Convert both mixed numbers into improper fractions:

11⁄5 = (1 × 5) + 1⁄5 = 6⁄5

21⁄3 = (2 × 3) + 1⁄3 = 7⁄3

2. Find a lowest common denominator for 5 and 3, which is 15:

6⁄5 = 6 × 3⁄5 × 3 = 18⁄15

7⁄3 = 7 × 5⁄3 × 5 = 35⁄15

3. Add the numerators together:

18⁄15 + 35⁄15 = 53⁄15

4. Convert back to a mixed number: 53 ÷ 15 = 3 remainder 8.

38⁄15

2.
(a) a = 3, b = 1
(b) Every prime factor in the prime factorisation of 33n has an even exponent, which makes it a perfect square.
Show workings

Given m = 2 × 3a × 7b and n = 3 × 72 × 11.

(a) Given that m > 126, find the smallest values of a and b which make m a multiple of 126:

Find the prime factorisation of 126:

126 = 2 × 63 = 2 × 32 × 71

For m to be a multiple of 126, its prime factor powers for 3 and 7 must be greater than or equal to those of 126:

Power of 3 must be at least 2 ⟹ a ≥ 2

Power of 7 must be at least 1 ⟹ b ≥ 1

If a = 2 and b = 1, then m = 2 × 32 × 71 = 126, which is NOT greater than 126.

So increase one exponent to make m the next smallest multiple:

a = 3, b = 1: m = 2 × 33 × 71 = 2 × 27 × 7 = 378

Check: 378 ÷ 126 = 3. ✓ And 378 > 126. ✓

a = 2, b = 2: m = 2 × 32 × 72 = 2 × 9 × 49 = 882 (larger)

So the smallest values are a = 3, b = 1.

(b) Explain why 33n is a perfect square:

Substitute the value of n into the expression:

33n = 33 × (3 × 72 × 11)

Break down 33 into prime factors (33 = 3 × 11):

33n = (3 × 11) × 3 × 72 × 11 = 32 × 72 × 112 = (3 × 7 × 11)2

Every prime factor in the prime factorisation of 33n has an even exponent, which makes it a perfect square.

Calculate the radius of the circle given PQ = 5 cm and QR = 12 cm:

1. Line segment PR passes through the centre O, making it a diameter. The angle subtended by a diameter at the circumference is always a right angle (∠PQR = 90°).

2. Apply Pythagoras' theorem to right-angled triangle △PQR to find diameter PR:

PR = √(PQ2 + QR2) = √(52 + 122) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 cm

3. Calculate the radius: Radius = Diameter⁄2 = 13⁄2 = 6.5 cm

10.
(a) 30°
(b) 12
Show workings

The ratio of interior angle to exterior angle of a regular polygon is 5 : 1.

(a) Find the size of an exterior angle:

Interior and exterior angles on any vertex lie on a straight line and sum to 180°.

Total parts = 5 + 1 = 6 parts

Size of 1 part (exterior angle) = 1⁄6 × 180° = 30°

(b) Calculate the number of sides of the polygon:

The sum of all exterior angles of a polygon is always 360°.

Number of sides = 360°⁄Exterior Angle = 360°⁄30° = 12

Calculate the height of the cuboid not in contact with water:

14.
(a) (a − 1 − b)(a − 1 + b)
(b) R = 3a + 2b
Show workings

(a) Factorise completely: (a − 1)2 − b2

Use the difference of two squares identity, A2 − B2 = (A − B)(A + B), where A = (a − 1) and B = b:

[(a − 1) − b][(a − 1) + b] = (a − 1 − b)(a − 1 + b)

(b) Make R the subject of the formula a⁄2 = R⁄6 − b⁄3:

Clear the denominators by multiplying the entire equation by the lowest common multiple, which is 6:

6 × (a⁄2) = 6 × (R⁄6) − 6 × (b⁄3)

3a = R − 2b

Isolate R by adding 2b to both sides:

R = 3a + 2b

16.
a = 120°, b = 60°
Show workings

Calculate angles marked a and b:

1. Triangle △AFE is equilateral, so all its interior angles are 60°. Thus, ∠AEF = 60°.

2. Since ABCD is a rectangle, line AD is parallel to BC.

3. Angles on straight line FEG sum to 180°. Thus, ∠a = 180° − ∠AEF = 180° − 60° = 120°.

4. By alternate interior angles across parallel horizontal lines AD ∥ BC, the angle ∠DEG = ∠b. Since ∠DEG and ∠a are supplementary angles on a straight line: ∠DEG = 180° − 120° = 60° ⟹ ∠b = 60°.

Expand and simplify (x − 1)2 − (x2 − 5):

3. Combine expressions and collect like terms:

(x2 − 2x + 1) − x2 + 5 = (x2 − x2) − 2x + (1 + 5) = −2x + 6

21.
(a) Shown below — x + y = 80
(b) x = 50
Show workings

(a) Given 3x + y = 180, show that x + y = 80:

1. Angles inside triangle △BCD must add up to 180°:

∠DBC + ∠BCD + ∠BDC = 180°

y + (y + 10) + (2x + 10) = 180

2. Collect and group like terms:

2x + 2y + 20 = 180

3. Subtract 20 from both sides:

2x + 2y = 160

4. Divide the entire equation by 2:

x + y = 80 (Proven)

(b) Find the value of x:

Set up the system of two linear equations:

  1. 3x + y = 180
  2. x + y = 80

Subtract equation (2) from equation (1) to eliminate variable y:

(3x − x) = 180 − 80 ⟹ 2x = 100 ⟹ x = 50
